## Canonical Statement

Nested service exposure MUST be assessed as indirect counterparty risk with explicit mapping of service chains and control responsibilities.

## Definition

This model maps multi-entity crypto service chains, identifies control ownership (CDD, Travel Rule, sanctions, monitoring), and evaluates indirect exposure over time.

## Why It Matters

Direct counterparties can mask downstream entities where actual risk and control gaps sit.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Institutions assume end-to-end control coverage, while nested provider changes and unknown sub-chains create unmanaged exposure.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to complex service-chain assessment in regulated banking environments in the EU/UK.

This entry does not provide legal advice and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
