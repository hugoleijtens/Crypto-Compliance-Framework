## Canonical Statement

Crypto exposure MUST be explicitly captured during onboarding and periodically refreshed through structured intake that supports portfolio-level assessment and evidence correlation.

## Definition

Crypto onboarding intake is the controlled collection of wallet identifiers, service relationships, portfolio scope, and evidence requests needed for CDD, SoF/SoW, and ODD.

## Why It Matters

Weak intake creates blind spots that propagate into monitoring, source analysis, and escalation quality.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Portfolio scope remains implicit, off-chain services are missed, and downstream controls operate on incomplete context.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to onboarding and periodic refresh workflows in regulated banking environments in the EU/UK.

This entry does not provide legal advice and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
