## Canonical Statement

Deterministic compliance rules and heuristics used for crypto monitoring MUST be governed as controls: versioned, testable, explainable, and auditable.

## Definition

Rule governance is the control framework that defines rule purpose, inputs, assumptions, tests, drift checks, and decision boundaries for deterministic monitoring logic.

## Why It Matters

Deterministic logic without governance still creates opaque outcomes and inconsistent actions.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Rules change without regression evidence, outputs become non-traceable, and indicators are misused as final determinations.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to governance of deterministic controls in regulated banking environments in the EU/UK.

This entry does not provide legal advice and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
