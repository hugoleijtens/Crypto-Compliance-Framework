## Canonical Statement

Crypto compliance outcomes MUST be supported by data lineage and data quality controls that allow reconstruction of inputs, transformations, and outputs across on-chain and off-chain sources.

## Definition

Data lineage in this framework is the traceable chain from source records through normalization, reconciliation, and aggregation to final compliance outputs, with versioned transformations and quality checks.

## Why It Matters

Without lineage, portfolio aggregation, scoring, and suspicion assessments are not auditable or reproducible.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Outputs cannot be traced to inputs, transformation logic becomes opaque, and unresolved data quality issues contaminate downstream decisions.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to regulated banking environments in the EU/UK and operational data-governance controls for crypto compliance.

This entry does not provide legal advice and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
