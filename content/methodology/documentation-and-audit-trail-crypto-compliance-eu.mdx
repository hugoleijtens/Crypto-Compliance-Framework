## Canonical Statement

Crypto compliance outcomes MUST be supported by an auditable trail that records evidence provenance, analytical steps, decisions, and residual risk, enabling independent review.

## Definition

Within this framework, an audit trail is the structured record of evidence sources, transformations, assumptions, decision rationale, and approvals needed to reconstruct compliance outcomes.

## Why It Matters

Without traceability, decisions cannot be defended. Auditability is a core control property, not an optional documentation step.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Evidence provenance is lost, assumptions remain implicit, and decision outcomes cannot be reconstructed under audit or supervisory scrutiny.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to regulated banking environments in the EU/UK and operational interpretation for audit-trail requirements in crypto compliance.

This entry does not provide legal advice and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
