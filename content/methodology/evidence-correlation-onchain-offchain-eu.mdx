## Canonical Statement

Compliance conclusions in crypto contexts MUST be derived from correlated on-chain and off-chain evidence where risk requires, using explicit linkage and explainable aggregation logic.

## Definition

Within this framework, evidence correlation is the documented process of linking transaction lineage, account records, and contextual records to produce reproducible conclusions and explicit gap statements.

## Why It Matters

On-chain and off-chain records answer different questions. Correlation is required to connect movement, identity, and economic purpose in a defensible way.

## Failure Mode if Ignored

Analyses rely on single-source assumptions, provenance is unclear, and unresolved linkage gaps are not captured as residual risk.

## Scope & Non-Claims

This entry is scoped to regulated banking environments in the EU/UK and operational interpretation for evidence correlation in crypto compliance.

This entry does not provide legal advice, does not guarantee completeness, and requires human validation for final compliance determinations.
